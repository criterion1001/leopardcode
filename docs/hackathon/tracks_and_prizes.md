# Open Agent Hackathon 2026 — Tracks, Prizes & Scoring

> Source: https://hackathon.genai.works/event/open-agent-hackathon-2026

## Challenge tracks — pick one, build for it

### Track 01 — Solving Fragmented Intelligence

Demonstrate a clever ability to discover and work across fragmented data
sources.

### Track 02 — The Agent That Can Explain Why

Build agents that investigate complex questions across multiple data sources,
connect findings, and produce evidence-backed answers or recommendations.

### Track 03 — Reasoning Architecture

Demonstrate a novel ability to turn data, knowledge, memory, and reasoning into
reliable decisions and actions.

### Bonus track — Wildcard [Tinkerer]: Bring Your Own Project

Bring an existing project, prototype or concept and extend it using sponsor
technology such as Meterless and Zetaris. Entries are judged on the same
criteria as the other tracks, must integrate Zetaris and Meterless, and are
scored on the new work rather than prior development.

Sponsors involved: Zetaris, Meterless.

Every submission selects exactly one primary track (see
[official_rules](official_rules.md) §03).

## Scoring — 100 points

| Criterion | Points | What it measures |
|---|---|---|
| Impact | 30 | Consistency with the challenge statements: how effectively the solution addresses a meaningful real-world problem for its intended users. |
| Technical | 20 | How well the solution is built, including its technical implementation, reliability and architecture. |
| Innovation | 15 | How original or creative the approach to solving the problem is. |
| Demo | 15 | How clearly the demo shows the solution, its functionality, and the value it provides to its intended users. |
| Product & UX | 10 | How intuitive, accessible, and easy to use the product is for its intended users. |
| Sponsor tech | 10 | How effectively the solution uses the sponsors' technology to address the problem and enhance the overall product. |

- Each submission is reviewed by at least three judges.
- Ties are resolved on the Impact score, then on submission timestamp.
