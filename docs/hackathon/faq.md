# Open Agent Hackathon 2026 — FAQ, Judges & Mentors

> Source: https://hackathon.genai.works/event/open-agent-hackathon-2026

## FAQ

### Does it cost anything to enter?

No. Registration, the platform workspace, mentor office hours and submission are
all free.

### I do not have a team yet — can I still register?

Yes. Register solo and use Team Finder on the platform; most teams form during
the first evening. You can also compete alone.

### Is it really fully online?

Yes. Kickoff, office hours, judging and results all happen online in Discord and
on the platform. No travel, no local chapter required.

### Can I use closed-source models and paid APIs?

Yes, including sponsor credits. Just declare anything pre-existing in your
submission so judges can see what you built during the window.

### What happens after I click Register?

One sign-up covers the event and community updates. Joining the Discord server is
optional — skip it if you don't use Discord. You land in your hackathon
workspace with the schedule, resources and submission form.

### How do students take part as a group?

University organisers can request a cohort code so every registration from a
campus is grouped together — email hackathons@genai.works.
