## The event

**Build an agent that does real work.**

The Open Agent Hackathon 2026 brings together developers, builders, and
innovators to create useful, real-world agentic applications that solve
meaningful problems for clearly defined users.

Over a six-day build sprint, participants explore how AI agents, data, and
modern developer tools can be combined to create functional products and
workflows. The hackathon provides access to sponsor technologies, resources,
workshops, and technical support to help teams move from an idea to a working
solution.

Participants work across a range of real-world use cases and challenge areas,
with a focus on building practical solutions rather than simple prototypes or
demonstrations. Teams are encouraged to make meaningful use of the available
sponsor technologies and clearly demonstrate how these technologies contribute
to their solution.

The hackathon is designed to encourage experimentation, collaboration, and
practical application of agentic AI while giving participants the opportunity to
showcase what they can build using emerging AI and data technologies.
